About

How I work

I'm a senior product designer who treats AI as a material, not a feature. It changes how products feel, what they can do, and how I work — every step of the way.

Fifteen years across Fintech, SaaS, Video Streaming, Food Tech and AgriTech. I've shipped consumer apps, complex B2B tools, and design systems used by teams of 50+.

Since 2023 my workflow has fundamentally changed: AI sits inside research, ideation, prototyping and even handoff. I design AI-powered products from the inside — knowing where models help, where they hurt, and how interaction patterns shift when the system can reason.

I work solo, embedded with one or two teams at a time, from discovery through engineering handoff.
